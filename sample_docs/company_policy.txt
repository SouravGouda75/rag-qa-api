Acme Corp Employee Handbook - Leave and Remote Work Policy
===========================================================

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Annual Leave
   All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 2 days per month. New employees can begin using accrued leave after completing their 90-day probationary period. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Any leave beyond 5 days that is not used by December 31 will be forfeited.

2. Sick Leave
   Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over and resets on January 1 each year. Employees must notify their manager before 9:00 AM on the day of absence.

3. Remote Work Policy
   Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 48 hours in advance. Employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM) regardless of work location. The company provides a monthly stipend of $150 for home office expenses to employees approved for regular remote work.

4. Parental Leave
   Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date. Employees must provide at least 30 days notice before the expected start of parental leave.

5. Public Holidays
   The company observes 10 public holidays per year as determined by the corporate calendar published in November for the following year. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday will be observed.

6. Leave Request Process
   All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal. Requests for 5 or more consecutive days require approval from both the direct manager and department head. The company reserves the right to deny leave requests during critical business periods with at least 2 weeks notice.
